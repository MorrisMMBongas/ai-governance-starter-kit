# NIST AI RMF Mapping

This table shows which parts of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) each template relates to. It shows where the templates line up with the framework. Using them doesn't make a company compliant or certified.

Checked against [NIST AI 100-1](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1) and the [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/) on [YYYY-MM-DD].

## Core templates

| Template | NIST function | Related categories and subcategories |
|---|---|---|
| AI use policy | Govern | GOVERN 1: Policies, processes, procedures and practices |
| AI use case check | Map, Govern | MAP 1: Context is established and understood<br>MAP 2: Categorization of the AI system<br>MAP 5: Impacts are characterized<br>GOVERN 6: Third-party risks (vendor row) |
| AI register | Govern | GOVERN 1.5: g and periodic review<br>GOVERN 1.6: Inventory of AI systems<br>GOVERN 1.7: Decommissioning AI systems (retired tools) |
| AI incident steps | Manage | MANAGE 2.4: Disengaging or deactivating AI systems<br>MANAGE 4.3: Tracking, responding to and communicating incidents |

## Optional templates

| Template | NIST function | Related categories and subcategories |
|---|---|---|
| Test plan | Measure | MEASURE 1: Methods and metrics<br>MEASURE 2: Evaluation for trustworthy characteristics |
| Generative AI risk checks | Map, Measure | The 12 risks in NIST AI 600-1 (Generative AI Profile) |
| Metrics | Measure | MEASURE 3: Tracking risks over time |
| Roles | Govern | GOVERN 2: Accountability structures |

## Not covered in this version

- **GOVERN 3:** Workforce diversity, equity, inclusion and accessibility
- **GOVERN 4:** Organizational culture around AI risk (partly covered by the policy and the incident steps)
- **GOVERN 5:** Engagement with people and groups outside the company
- **MANAGE 3:** Detailed management of third-party risks (only partly covered by the vendor row)
