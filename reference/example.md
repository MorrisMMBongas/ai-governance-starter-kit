# Example

This is a made-up example. It isn't based on a real company or product.

An online store with 40 staff wants AI to **draft replies to customer emails**. A support agent edits every draft before sending.

## AI use case check
| Area | Rating | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Data | High | Customer names and order details |
| Impact if wrong | Medium | Wrong reply causes cost and complaints, but can be fixed |
| Human check | Low | Every draft reviewed |
| Who can send input | High | Customers write the emails |
| Vendor | Medium | Data location not yet confirmed |
| **Risk level** | **High** | At least one row is High |

**Decision:** Approved with conditions by leadership.
**Conditions:**
- The vendor confirms in writing that it doesn't train on customer data.
- Drafts are never sent automatically.
- The tool is tested with emails that contain hidden instructions.
- There's a spending limit and a way to turn it off.
