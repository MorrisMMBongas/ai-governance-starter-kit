# AI Use Policy

**Owner:** [Name / role] · **Approved by:** [Name / role] · **Effective:** [Date] · **Next review:** [Date]

This policy covers anyone who uses AI tools for work, and any product feature that uses AI.

## What you can do
You can use the tools listed in the [AI register](3-ai-register.md) for drafting, summaries, coding help and research.
Check the output before you rely on it.

## What needs approval first
Fill in the [AI use case check](2-ai-use-case-check.md) before you:
- Use a tool that isn't in the AI register
- Use personal, customer or confidential data
- Show AI output to customers or put it in a product
- Use AI to make or influence decisions about people
- Let AI send, change, buy or delete anything without a person checking first

## What isn't allowed
- Entering passwords, keys or other secrets into an AI tool
- Entering personal or confidential data into a tool that isn't approved for it
- Saying AI output has been checked when it hasn't

## Who is responsible
Whoever uses or ships AI output is responsible for it.

## Reporting problems
If something goes wrong, follow the [AI incident steps](4-ai-incident-steps.md). You won't get in trouble for reporting a problem honestly.
