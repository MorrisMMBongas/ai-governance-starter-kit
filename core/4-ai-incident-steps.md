# AI Incident Steps

## What counts as an AI incident
- Personal, confidential or secret data shared with or exposed by AI
- Harmful or misleading AI output reached a customer or the public
- AI took an action that caused damage
- Someone tricked the AI into breaking its rules

## What to do
1. **Stop it.** Turn off the feature or stop using the tool.
2. **Report it** to [name / channel] within [24 hours], or [1 hour] if customers or personal data are affected.
3. **Assess it.** The owner and [security lead] confirm the impact and decide who else must be told.
4. **Fix it.** Correct the cause.
5. **Prevent it.** Update the policy, use case check or register so it doesn't happen again.
