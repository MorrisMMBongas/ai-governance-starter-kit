# Roles

| Role | Who | Responsible for |
|---|---|---|
| AI tool owner | [ ] | Use case check, register entry, testing, reviews |
| Approver (low risk) | [Team lead] | Approving low-risk use |
| Approver (medium risk) | [Security lead] | Approving medium-risk use |
| Approver (high risk) | [Leadership or AI review group] | Approving high-risk use |
| Incident contact | [ ] | Receiving and coordinating incident reports |
| Policy owner | [ ] | Keeping the policy and this kit up to date |

One person can have more than one role, but nobody should approve their own medium or high-risk request.
